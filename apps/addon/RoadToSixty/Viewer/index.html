Road to Sixty

Drop your journeys here

  1. In game, type /reload or log out. That is when WoW writes saved data to disk.
  2. Drag in your whole WTF folder, found at World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF (or _classic_era_\WTF, _anniversary_\WTF).
  3. From a friend: they send RoadToSixty.lua from WTF\Account\<account>\SavedVariables. Drop those in too, as many friends as you like.

Everything stays on this PC. Files are read as data, nothing in them is run.

# Road to Sixty

**Road to Sixty** quietly records where your character goes from level 1 to 60. Open the map whenever you like to see your whole adventure drawn across Azeroth, and replay it from the very first step.

## Features

### Your path on the world map
- One map for the whole world: zoom smoothly from Azeroth into each continent and down to terrain detail.
- The path changes colour as you level, using the item quality colours: **white**, **green**, **blue**, **purple**, **orange**.
- Flight paths and corpse runs have their own styles, and arcane sparkles drift along the road.

### Replay
- Press **Play** to watch your journey unfold, at **1x to 32x** speed.
- The timeline bar shows your level bands. Click or drag it to jump to any moment, and hover it to see your level, zone and date at that point.

### Every jump explained
- **Hearthstones**, **mage teleports**, **boats**, **deaths** and **dungeons** are drawn as coloured arcs, with an icon when you zoom in.
- Hover any line for details, for example *"Boat: Stormwind Harbor to Auberdine"*.

### History
- A side panel lists your level ups, deaths, dungeon runs (with a summary of each run), flights, hearths, teleports, boats, new zones, and **green, blue and epic loot**.
- Filters hide a category from both the list and the map.
- During a replay, the History list follows along and highlights where you are in the story.

### Stats
- Totals and a level-by-level table: kills, quests, experience, deaths, time played, distance walked and distance flown.

### Your other characters
- A Characters tab shows all your characters and where they last were.
- Turn on their paths to see them alongside your own.

### Light on performance
- Recording is lightweight. The map only draws detail where you are looking, so zooming stays smooth even with a full 1–60 journey.

## Commands

Click the **minimap button** to open the map (drag it to move it), or use:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/rts map` | Open the journey map |
| `/rts check` | Check that recording is working |
| `/rts stats` | Show journey totals and recording size |
| `/rts where` | Show your position as the recorder sees it |
| `/rts minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/rts motes` | Turn the arcane sparkles on the path on or off |
| `/rts greet` | Turn the login message on or off |
| `/rts reset confirm` | Erase this character's journey |

## Good to know
- The game saves addon data when you **log out** or **/reload**. If the game crashes, the last part of that session may be lost.
- Recording starts when you install the addon. Earlier travels cannot be recovered, so install it early!
- Journeys are stored per character. The Characters tab shows all of them on your account.

## Description

Road to Sixty quietly records where your character goes from level 1 to 60, then replays the whole adventure on one map of Azeroth.

⬇️ Download free on CurseForge:
https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/road-to-sixty

What it does:
🗺️ Draws your path across the world map, coloured by level: white, green, blue, purple, orange
▶️ Replays your journey from the first step, at 1x to 32x speed
⚔️ Remembers your gear at every level up, and shows it changing during the replay
🚢 Shows hearthstones, flight paths, boats, deaths and dungeon runs, each with its own style
📜 Keeps a History of level ups, deaths, dungeons, new zones and green, blue and epic loot
📊 Gives level-by-level stats: kills, quests, deaths, time played, distance walked and flown
👥 Lets you see your other characters' paths alongside your own

How to use it:
Install it, play as normal, and type /rts in game (or click the minimap button) to open your journey.

Recording starts when you install the addon, so install it early!

This video replays a sample level 1-30 warrior journey through Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, Westfall, Redridge, Duskwood, the Wetlands, Darkshore and Ashenvale. It uses generated test data, so the paths zigzag more than a real character's would. Your own journey will be much more accurate, following the roads, quest hubs and flight paths you actually took.
